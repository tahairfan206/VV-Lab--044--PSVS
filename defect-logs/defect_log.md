# PSVS Inspection Defect Log

| Defect ID | Req ID | Category | Description | Evidence / Rationale | Severity | Suggested Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DEF-01** | S1 | Incompleteness | Undefined error behavior for unauthorized deposit attempts. | S1 specifies acceptance criteria only; omits logging and rejection workflows. | Major | "The system shall reject deposit requests from unregistered entities, log the security event, and notify security." | Open |
| **DEF-02** | S2 | Redundancy | Wholly subsumed by S3 chamber capacities. | S2 asserts chambers hold "a large number", which S3 specifies with concrete integer capacities. | Minor | Deprecate S2 entirely in favor of S3. | Open |
| **DEF-03** | S2 | Vagueness | Imprecise quantifier "large number". | Subjective term lacks numerical bounds. | Minor | Remove S2 or define aggregate capacity across all chambers. | Open |
| **DEF-04** | S3 | Incompleteness | Missing overflow handling when chamber limit is reached. | No policy defined when a specific chamber hits 50,000 or 20,000 boxes. | Major | "If Chamber $i$ reaches capacity, the system shall prohibit further deposits into Chamber $i$." | Open |
| **DEF-05** | S4, S5 | Contradiction | Mutual exclusion between sole depositor ownership and emergency third-party release. | S4 mandates *only* depositor may withdraw; S5 allows release to *any* requesting party. | Critical | "A box may only be withdrawn by its depositor, except under a declared Level-3 Emergency per Vault Charter §4." | Open |
| **DEF-06** | S5 | Ambiguity | Undefined threshold for what constitutes an "emergency". | Leaves critical access control vulnerable to arbitrary invocation. | Critical | "An emergency shall be defined strictly as a formal declaration under PSVS Operational Protocol OP-901." | Open |
| **DEF-07** | S5 | Untraceability | Untraceable recipient entity "any requesting party". | Exposes custodial assets to arbitrary third parties without legal qualification. | Critical | Replace "any requesting party" with explicit authorized legal entities (e.g., designated secondary gene bank). | Open |
| **DEF-08** | S6 | Vagueness | Unquantified thermal threshold "safe temperature". | "Safe temperature" has no thermal units, tolerances, or reference standard. | Major | Remove S6 and let specific thermal limits (S7) govern storage conditions. | Open |
| **DEF-09** | S7, S8 | Contradiction | Direct clash between absolute temperature ceiling and maintenance warm-up. | S7 uses absolute "never" rise above -18°C; S8 warms the chamber to 0°C. | Critical | "Except during scheduled maintenance conforming to S8, chamber temperature shall not exceed -18°C." | Open |
| **DEF-10** | S8 | Incompleteness | Lacks safety evacuation rule and duration limits for 0°C warm-up. | Warming to 0°C with seeds inside causes thermal and condensation damage. | Critical | "During maintenance, boxes must be evacuated to an active chamber prior to warming to 0°C for at most 72 hours." | Open |
| **DEF-11** | S9 | Infeasibility | Cannot test internal seed viability inside sealed boxes non-destructively. | Violates the physical and legal constraint that vault staff do not open sealed boxes. | Critical | "Viability monitoring shall be based strictly on depositor-supplied shelf-life metadata and elapsed storage time." | Open |
| **DEF-12** | S9 | Vagueness | Unquantified threshold "low viability" and duration "promptly". | Neither numerical viability floor nor notification timeline is defined. | Major | "When calculated viability drops below 85%, the system shall issue an alert within 24 hours." | Open |
| **DEF-13** | S10 | Incompleteness | Lacks rounding rules for fractional withdrawal quotas. | For a balance of 3 boxes, 20% equals 0.6 boxes. System action is undefined. | Major | "The 20% annual limit shall be rounded down to the nearest integer, with a minimum allowance of 1 box." | Open |
| **DEF-14** | S10, S5 | Contradiction | Emergency releases violate annual species withdrawal quotas. | S10 sets strict 20% quota; S5 allows full release without respecting limits. | Major | "Annual withdrawal quotas (S10) shall apply exclusively to standard depositor-initiated requests." | Open |
| **DEF-15** | S11 | Incompleteness | Unspecified criteria and protocols for "re-inspection". | Fails to state inspection standards, passing criteria, or handling of failed re-inspections. | Major | "A redeposited box must pass physical seal verification (Standard SOP-12) prior to storage allocation." | Open |
| **DEF-16** | S12 | Non-Verifiability | Qualitative phrase "good audit trail" cannot be objectively verified. | "Good" has no testable or mathematical acceptance criteria. | Major | "The system shall log all operations to an append-only, tamper-evident log conforming to ISO/IEC 27001." | Open |
